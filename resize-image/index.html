How to use it

  1. Add your images.
  2. Choose how to resize, or tap a social media size.
  3. Download the resized images.

Resize an image to the exact size you need

Pick a width, a height or a percentage, and Slicemat scales every image you add. Use Width or Height to keep the proportions. Use Fit inside a box when the image must not be larger than a given size, and Fill a box and crop when the result must match the box exactly.

Resize an image for Instagram, Stories and YouTube

Tap a social size and Slicemat fills the frame and trims the extra from the edges. The presets cover 1080×1080 for a square post, 1080×1350 for a portrait post, 1080×1920 for Stories, 1280×720 for a YouTube thumbnail and 1200×630 for a link preview on Facebook.

Make the file smaller as well

Fewer pixels usually mean a smaller file. If you need a specific size such as 100 KB, resize first and then run the photo through the image compressor. To cut one wide picture into several posts, use the image splitter instead.

Everything stays on your device

Slicemat resizes inside your browser. Your photos are never sent to a server, and you can resize a whole batch and download it as one ZIP.

Questions people ask

How do I resize an image without stretching it?

Use Width or Height and keep the other side empty in your mind: Slicemat keeps the proportions for you. Fit inside a box also keeps proportions.

What does Fill a box and crop do?

It scales the image until the box is full, then trims the extra from the edges. It suits Instagram, Story and thumbnail sizes.

Can I resize many images at once?

Yes. Add them all and every image gets the same settings. Download them together as a ZIP.

Does resizing upload my photos?

No. Your images are processed in your browser and never sent to a server.
