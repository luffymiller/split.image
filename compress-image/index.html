Reduce an image from MB to KB

A phone photo often weighs 3 to 8 MB. One MB equals 1024 KB, so a 4 MB photo is about 4096 KB. Many online forms, job portals and email systems accept far less, and big images also slow a website down. Slicemat shrinks your photo to the size you pick without a sign-up and without uploading it anywhere.

How to compress a photo to 100 KB

Add the photo, tap 100 KB, and download the result. Slicemat tries the highest quality that still fits under 100 KB. If the photo is still too big at that point, it shrinks the dimensions step by step until it fits. You see the size before and after, so you know exactly what you got. You can open this page with a size already chosen: 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB.

Which format gives the smallest file?

JPG is the best choice for photos and works in every app. WebP often saves another 20 to 30 percent and can keep transparency, but some apps cannot open it, so check where you will upload the file first. PNG is lossless and heavy, which is why Slicemat turns PNG photos into JPG or WebP when you set a target size.

Size limits you will run into

  • Government and job application forms often ask for a photo or signature between 20 KB and 200 KB. Read the exact number on the form.
  • Email attachments feel faster below 1 MB, and many inboxes cap the total attachment size.
  • Website images load fastest under 200 KB, especially on mobile data.

If the file is still too large after compression, make it smaller in pixels first with the image resizer. A photo that is 1200 pixels wide reaches 100 KB with far less quality loss than a 4000 pixel original.

Does compression upload my photo?

No. The compression runs inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

Questions people ask

How do I reduce a photo to 100 KB?

Add your photo, tap 100 KB in the target list and download the result. Slicemat tries the highest quality that stays under the target. If that is not enough, it shrinks the image a little.

Will compressing make my image look worse?

A smaller file always loses some detail. For most photos, you will not notice the difference at moderate targets. Very small targets such as 20 KB can look soft.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The compression happens inside your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why did a PNG turn into a JPG?

JPG and WebP shrink photos far better than PNG. Choose WebP in the format options if you need to keep transparency.
