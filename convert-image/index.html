Convert JPG, PNG and WebP in your browser

Choose the format you want, drop in your images, and download the converted files. You can convert PNG to JPG, JPG to WebP, WebP to JPG or WebP to PNG, one file or a whole folder at a time.

Which format should you pick?

  • JPG suits photos and works in every app, including Instagram and WhatsApp.
  • PNG suits screenshots, logos and anything with transparent areas. Its files are large.
  • WebP gives the smallest files for websites. Some older apps and social networks cannot open it.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

JPG cannot store transparency, so Slicemat fills transparent areas with white when you save as JPG. PNG and WebP keep them. Converting a WebP image to JPG is the quickest fix when an app refuses to open a WebP file.

Need a smaller file after converting?

Send the converted image through the image compressor and pick a target such as 200 KB. Your files stay on your device the whole time.
